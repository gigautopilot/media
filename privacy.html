Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

GigAutopilot (“we”) publishes short educational content about AI tools on Instagram, Threads and YouTube under the handle @gigautopilot. This policy explains how our internal tool, GigAutopilot Manager, handles data.

What the app does

GigAutopilot Manager is used only by the GigAutopilot team to manage our own brand accounts: publish our posts and videos, read and reply to comments on our posts, and read performance statistics of our own content.

Data we access

  • Our own account profile information and our own posts and videos.
  • Comments that people leave on our posts, including the commenter’s public username and comment text, so we can read and reply to them.
  • Aggregated statistics for our own content (views, likes, comments, shares, saves).

We do not access private messages, contacts, or any account other than our own.

How we use and store data

Data is used only to publish our content, reply to comments and measure what works. Access tokens and statistics are stored on our own computer. We do not sell, rent or share this data with third parties and do not use it for advertising.

Google user data

GigAutopilot Manager’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is accessed through the YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept for as long as we need them to plan our content. You can revoke the app’s access to a Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To ask us to delete any data related to you, email us and we will do so within 30 days.
